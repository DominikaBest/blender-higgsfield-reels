# Directing an AI shot with a phone

**Blender × VirtuCam × Higgsfield Seedance 2.5.** A 15-second single take: a rider gallops toward a camera on a tripod, grabs it, and films herself while a herd overtakes her. The camera move was performed on an iPhone inside a grey Blender blockout. Seedance 2.5 rendered the film from that playblast plus two reference stills.

Open `index.html` (or the GitHub Pages link) for the full write-up: the final film next to the Blender take, the process step by step, the references, the prompt changes, and what broke along the way.
